## Course Flow

During this workshop, we will be creating an interactive dashboard with a number of panels that display different data related to evaluating targets in drug discovery. We will all start by creating a panel, whose instructions are found in `PANEL_A.md`. You can open up that file to view the instructions that the AI agent will follow.

To create panel A, click into the chat window on the right and enter "Let's get started. Create panel A." This will construct scratch an interactive dashboard displaying GWAS data for our target of interest.

After that, we will create panel b, which shows the structure of our target of interest. After panel B is incorporated into our dashboard, let's edit the instructions file for panel B to discover a new data source that displays the binding domain of the molecule of interest.

Figuring out the *best* directions to give the AI agent is the purpose of this workshop. You will collaborate with your small group and your agent to provide the most helpful instructions for the AI agent.

After panel b, work through the rest of the panels in you small group, starting with panel c. After that, they do not need to be done in order. You can jump around to the topics that interest you.

> **Tip**: are you an expert user and want to make things more difficult? Before proceeding with the rest of the panels, change the target of interest to something else.
